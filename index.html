FAQ

How the academy will work.

Are classes online?

Yes. The platform is designed for live online classes, recorded lessons and downloadable learning materials.

Can students join for individual topics?

Yes. Courses can be structured as full programmes, chapter-wise learning or targeted one-to-one support.

Will there be an app?

Yes. The first release is designed as a mobile-friendly web app/PWA, with a future path to dedicated Android and iOS applications.

Will study materials and tests be available?

Yes. The planned platform includes notes, question banks, previous-year questions, assignments and online tests.
